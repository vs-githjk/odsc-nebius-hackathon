Patient Zero, three directions

Surface: the web app a reporter opens with a viral number. The one feeling: the floor drops as the sentence you trusted rewinds into something else. Six signature features in every direction: the time-machine scrubber (drag the handle, the sentence morphs word by word while the tree rewinds), blame for a sentence (hover any word), the distortion meter (typed readouts with confidence and an "unsure" floor), trace from any page, the Zombie Stat Atlas, and researcher mode.

Constraints held in all three. Counter wording "traced to N roots we could link"; the red hop reads "earliest page we found with the new wording"; five stop states; LIVE and REPLAY badges with every example as REPLAY; red never alone (icon plus text); no private individuals. Example data. Only the 2012 rows are VERIFIED Wayback text. Every other wording, count, date and confidence on this page is an example for layout. Scrubber. Drag it in every direction; keyboard arrows work; with reduced motion each state snaps to its end frame.

Case File. A research instrument on paper: one deep ink, one forest action, red reserved for the hop where the words changed. The scrubber is a microfilm reel: drag it and the viral line reflows into the 2012 rows in a text serif while the tree's branches withdraw into the archive.

Palette


paper, ink, forest action, mutation red, mint good, amber caution; dark theme on soot with mint action

Type

Newsreader (quoted wordings, hero numbers, headings) with Geist (interface, log, labels). Serif only where words are evidence.

Motion language

Archival: cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.3,1); words slide 320 ms and fade 220 ms; nodes fade and shrink 520 ms; edges draw 700 ms. Only evidence moves (words, nodes, edges, meter bars); chrome never does. Reduced motion: same end state, no transitions.

Draws on

Wayback Machine and Bloomberg time rails for the axis; the Pudding and Reuters Graphics for annotated trees; court exhibit sheets for the origin card; the quick look Vidyut liked, pushed to a full instrument.

Why it is the safe push. Vidyut has seen and liked this world; this round makes the scrubber the hero and adds the four other features in the same grammar.

Instrument. A seismograph for sentences. Cool stone ground, panels on a fine grid, one signal amber for the playhead and the needle, mono readouts that count. The scrubber is a tape shuttle across a strip chart of copies per year; the sentence sits in flap tiles that step when the tape passes a hop.

Palette


stone ground, ink navy, signal amber, mutation red, good green, grid; dark theme on deep navy with brighter amber

Type

IBM Plex Mono (sentence tiles, readouts, log, badges) with IBM Plex Sans (prose, labels). Uppercase mono labels with .08em tracking.

Motion language

Mechanical: linear playhead, steps(6) flap on word change over 240 ms, readouts count with tabular numerals, needles ease-out 600 ms on cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), bars collapse 320 ms linear. Nothing eases in and out softly; every move reads as a measurement. Reduced motion: values snap.

Draws on

Seismograph and ECG strip charts; Teenage Engineering panels; Linear's data density; the Wayback calendar; aircraft annunciator panels for the stop state.

Telephone. The children's game, drawn big. White canvas, black ink, one tangerine for the changed words and one ultramarine for blame. The claim is poster-sized type; below it a chain of five speech cards, one per page that passed the sentence on. The scrubber is a fat pill that drags the whole chain back to the first whisper.

Palette


white, ink, tangerine (changed words, mutation), ultramarine (blame, links), mint good, grey tiles; dark theme on charcoal

Type

Schibsted Grotesk 800 for the claim and headings (normal width, reads at a glance), 400 and 500 for prose, Geist Mono for years, counts and badges.

Motion language

Playful but short: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), 480 ms; cards settle with a 1.5 degree tilt as they leave; words scale from .92 and slide; the changed word flashes tangerine once (240 ms). Hover lifts a tile 3 px. No bounce, no loops. Reduced motion: cards and words appear in place.
