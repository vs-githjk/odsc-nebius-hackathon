A · bold swing · recommended by planner and auditor

Patient Zero

Paste a viral stat or quote. It hunts the claim backwards through every article that repeated it, draws the family tree over time, and circles the exact hop where the words changed.

91 /100 auditor · 95 planner
  1. Paste "Humans have a shorter attention span than a goldfish: 8 seconds." Click Trace.
  2. The tree grows back from 2026 to 2012; 37 reprints of one story collapse into one node.
  3. Header: 142 articles. 1 independent source. 0 studies. The last node goes grey: trail goes cold.
  4. Click the red ring: the two quotes side by side, with "comparison added" highlighted.
For
Reporters, comms teams, research integrity, anyone about to share a stat
Proof
Hit rate on 30 claims whose origins human fact-checkers already published
Risk
The web may not link back far enough, and the tree comes out shallow
Spike
Week 0, about 6 h: trace 5 known claims; pass if 3 of 5 reach the origin under 150 Tavily credits and $0.50 each

Auditor: FIX, recommended. Ambition 4/5, creativity 4/5, rubric 91. Judges would screenshot the red hop. Before the spike counts, it must not peek at fact-check sites, must show traces 3 or more hops deep, and must date pages by their first archive capture. These go into the plan.

B · bold swing · stopped by the auditor

Objection!

Ask a contested question. A prosecution agent and a defense agent each build a sourced case, cross-examine each other's exhibits, and a judge rules only on evidence that survives, then says what would flip the verdict.

ADMITTEDEXCLUDED: QUOTE NOT ON PAGECONTEXT STRIPPEDMERGED: SAME PRESS RELEASE
78 /100 auditor · 88 planner
  1. Ask "Did return-to-office mandates raise productivity?" The screen splits: prosecution, defense, verdict meter at 50/50.
  2. Exhibits stream in and get stamped: admitted, excluded, merged.
  3. Cross-examination opens exhibit P4: the next sentence says "among call-centre staff only". Context stripped; the meter swings.
  4. The judge rules and names what would flip it. Click Order discovery and both sides search live.
For
Analysts writing bull and bear cases, policy and consulting teams, anyone deciding a contested health or money question
Proof
Ablation: court vs single agent vs neutral checker, same models, planted errors
Risk
Debate is a common default idea and a courtroom paper (PROClaim) exists; theatre without a measured gain loses points
Spike
Week 0, about 8 h: pass if cross-examination catches at least 5 more of 20 planted errors than a neutral checker

Auditor: STOP. Ambition 4/5 but creativity 2/5, rubric 78. At least five public "AI courtroom" repos exist, four pushed this month, and Objection Inc. already sells LLM-panel adjudication of disputed facts. The courtroom paper's own test credits most of its gain to better retrieval, not the roles.
