   https://www.who.int/news/item/12-07-2017-2-1-billion-people-lack-safe-drinking-water-at-home-more-than-twice-as-many-lack-safe-sanitation

**Where the wording changed**

- **2017 to 2019:** "3 in 10 (2.1 billion)" became "1 in 3 (2.2 billion)". The 2019 release changed the rounding and the headline. The underlying figure moved only from 2.1 to 2.2 billion. At roughly 7.5 billion people, 2.2 billion is about 29%, which is nearer 3 in 10 than 1 in 3. That arithmetic is mine, not the JMP's.
- **2019 onward:** "do not have safely managed drinking water services" became "do not have access to safe drinking water" in the headline. "Safely managed" is a strict technical standard: water on premises, free from contamination, and available when needed. Most people without it have a basic improved source. The 2019 release itself says only about 1 in 10 lacked even basic service.
- **Later reposts:** some, such as Data4SDGs, garbled it to "only one in three people do not have access".
- **Unrelated numbers mixed in:** The Barbecue Lab page, for instance, sits next to a "2.5 billion / 35%" sanitation figure, a different measure.

**It is now outdated**

The JMP's August 2025 report (published 26 August 2025) puts the figure at 2.1 billion people. Its headline is "1 in 4", reflecting coverage rising from 68% to 74% between 2015 and 2024. A January 2026 WHO/UNICEF update repeats the 2.1 billion figure. Anything still saying "1 in 3" is repeating a 2019 data release.
https://www.who.int/news/item/26-08-2025-1-in-4-people-globally-still-lack-access-to-safe-drinking-water---who--unicef
