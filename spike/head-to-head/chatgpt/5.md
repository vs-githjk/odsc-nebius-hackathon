## 2. Where the wording changed

The evolution is revealing:

By 2008 — Wikipedia

The record is described as having an approximately 85% fatality rate since 1940, without specifying a precise denominator.

2013 — WKMS

The subject changes from record attempts to the sport, which implies the statistic may apply to boat racing generally.

2015 — element14

The denominator becomes drivers making a “serious attempt,” while the author asserts eleven fatalities. The criteria for inclusion remain unspecified.

2016 — Ken Warby / ABC News

The percentage is given by an experienced record holder, but without a method of calculation.

2024 — Guinness World Records

A historical count uses identifiable participants and provides both numerator and denominator: seven of 13 since 1930.

The most consequential change was the shift between record contenders, serious attempts, all racers and the sport. These are different populations, and a fatality percentage cannot be compared meaningfully without specifying which is being counted.

The frequently cited 85% fatality rate lacks a verifiable calculation and should not be presented as an established statistic.
