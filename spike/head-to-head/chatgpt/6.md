## 3. What does the original experiment establish?

The trial randomized 206 pregnant women to receive a phosphatidylcholine supplement or placebo, starting at approximately 16 weeks of pregnancy.

The researchers assessed children's behavior and cognitive development through age four.

IQ was measured using the Wechsler Preschool and Primary Scale of Intelligence, Fourth Edition. In the reported comparison:

AVERAGE FULL-SCALE IQ DIFFERENCE

# +7.78 points

95% confidence interval: +1.88 to +13.70 points

Illustration of the reported estimate and confidence interval, measured in IQ points. The interval describes uncertainty about the average difference, not the scores of individual children.

The authors reported a p-value of 0.010 for this difference. That is statistical evidence of a group difference under the study's analysis, but it does not guarantee that every child benefits or that the average effect would be identical in another population.&#x20;
